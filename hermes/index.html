Hermes

Hermes is a private, single-user assistant that Eli runs on his own computer. It is not a public product and no one else can sign in to it.

Hermes uses Google sign-in to work with Eli's own Google account (ej.clawdbot@gmail.com):

  • Google Calendar — read appointments, add reminders, and keep a calendar of local game-store events in sync.
  • Gmail — read and organise Eli's own mail and, only with his approval, send messages on his behalf.

See the privacy policy for exactly what data is accessed and how it is handled.
