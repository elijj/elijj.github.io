Hermes privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Hermes is a personal assistant used only by its owner, Eli, with his own Google account. This policy describes how it handles Google user data.

Data accessed

  • Google Calendar: calendars, events and event details.
  • Gmail: messages, labels and metadata; sending mail when the owner approves it.

How the data is used

Only to carry out tasks the owner asks for — for example finding an appointment, creating a reminder, or syncing event listings to a calendar. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training models, and it is not sold or shared with anyone.

Where the data goes

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. When the owner asks Hermes a question, the relevant calendar or email content may be sent to the AI model provider the owner has configured, solely to produce the answer.

Limited Use

Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which deletes Hermes's ability to read or change anything.
